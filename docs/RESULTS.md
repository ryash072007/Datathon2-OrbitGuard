## Limitations

- Forecasts a later ESA risk estimate, not an observed collision.
- Historical anonymised ESA encounters are distinct from synthetic manoeuvre scenarios.
- Final labels are public retrospective labels; no operational live validation.
- Risk values are log10 probabilities; score clipping is benchmark-only.
- High-risk events are scarce in the eligible training split.
- ESA hand-picked the challenge splits to over-represent high-risk encounters; metrics do not estimate real-world encounter prevalence.
- Simulator: two-body gravity, impulsive tangential burns, prescribed (not propagated) RTN uncertainty; decision support only, not flight software.
